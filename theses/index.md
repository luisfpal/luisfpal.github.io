<h2>Data management</h2>
<p>One service layer over a research facility, built twice. People can govern where files live, and they can submit an image, wait, and get a classification back. Both versions were deployed and worked. They were not tuned for throughput. A paper on this, with Federica Bazzocchi and Tommaso Rodani, was submitted to IEEE eScience 2026 and was not accepted. The review wanted throughput experiments the paper did not have.</p>
